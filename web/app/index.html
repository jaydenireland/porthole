About Porthole

Getting ready

Downloading the Tailscale client for this tab.

Connect this tab to your tailnet

Sign in with Tailscale to add this tab to your tailnet as a device. It can reach what your account can reach, and nothing passes through a server of ours.

Preparing sign-in…

Finish signing in on the Tailscale page. This tab will continue on its own.

After you sign in, Porthole opens .

Leave this off on a shared computer. When it is on, this browser keeps the keys to your tailnet and your logins to its sites until you disconnect, and shows up as a lasting device that you can remove in the Tailscale admin console.

Waiting for approval

Your tailnet requires an admin to approve new devices. Ask an admin to approve this one in the Tailscale admin console; this tab will continue on its own.

Porthole stopped

The connection to your tailnet ended.
