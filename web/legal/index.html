Privacy

Porthole has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and sets no tracking cookies.

  • Your tailnet's pages. They travel encrypted between the tab and your devices, through Tailscale's relays. They do not pass through this site's host, and we cannot see them.
  • What the site's host sees. The files are served by Cloudflare. As with any website, Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details when the files are fetched. Each tailnet site is shown on its own address under this domain, and that address contains the site's name (for example your device's and tailnet's names), so those names are visible to Cloudflare and to your DNS provider when the address is first opened. The paths and contents of the pages are not.
  • What Tailscale sees. The same as for any device you add: the tab registers with Tailscale's coordination server and sends its encrypted traffic through Tailscale's relays. Tailscale's privacy policy covers that.
  • What is kept in your browser. The tab's device keys and your logins to tailnet sites are kept in the browser's storage for the session, or until you disconnect if you tick "Stay signed in on this browser". Nothing is copied anywhere else. This storage is needed for Porthole to work and is not used to track you.
  • Test pages. If you open a test page with ?report added, it sends its result and your browser's version to this site's log. Nothing is sent otherwise.

Questions about privacy, or a request about data relating to you, can be raised through the project's page on GitHub.
